## What you get

Live captions for your meetings — Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Google Meet or any call on your Mac — in a panel that floats above the call.

- **Live Captions** in the language the meeting is spoken, as it is spoken — the words drawn as they're said by the speech model, or by a lighter one you choose in Settings, Words While Speaking.
- **Speaker identification**: colleagues you saved a voice profile for are named; everyone else is Speaker 1, 2, 3, and your own lines are Me.
- **Translation on request**: translate a caption from the button it shows when you point at it, or turn on **Live Translation** to translate every new caption — with Apple Translation on this Mac, or the AI model you set up in Lumi.
- **Every meeting saved as a transcript** in the Meeting Notes window, to read again, copy, or export as Markdown or plain text.

## Private by design

Transcription, speaker identification and Apple Translation run on this Mac. Nothing leaves it unless you choose an online model in Lumi, and Lumi asks before anything is sent. Transcripts — and each meeting's audio, kept to listen back to unless you turn Keep Audio off in Settings — are encrypted, excluded from backups, and removed with Meeting Notes.

## Getting started

1. Install, and Meeting Notes opens on Setup: download the English speech model and the speaker identification model.
2. In your next meeting, choose **Start Transcription** from Meeting Notes' menu bar item.
3. Let the participants know the meeting is being transcribed.

Needs Lumi 1.95 or later. Apple Speech and Apple Translation need macOS 26; before it, Lumi Models' Whisper transcribes and the AI model you set up in Lumi translates. System audio needs macOS 14.4.

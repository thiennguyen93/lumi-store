Sticky Notes puts a note one key away, floating above whatever you are working in — and lets you leave a few of them on the desktop, beside your work, for as long as you need them.

## What you get

- **A note on a key.** `⌃⌥⌘N` starts a new one, `⌃⌥⌘S` brings the note back — or puts it away when you are in it — and `⌃⌥⌘F` finds one. Change any of them on the Shortcuts tab.
- **On top while you work.** The note stays above every app, full-screen ones included, without taking the app out of front. Turn Float on Top off and it goes away when you click elsewhere.
- **Notes on the desktop.** `⌘D` puts a note beside your work, where it stays until you put it back — up to 16 at once. `⌃⌥⌘S` brings them all back, where you left them.
- **Format as you type.** `# ` a heading, `[] ` a to-do, `- ` a list, `> ` a quote, `---` a line, or `/` for everything. The format bar and the usual keys work too: `⌘B`, `⌘I`, `⇧⌘S`, `⌘E`, `⌘L`.
- **Pictures.** Paste a screenshot, drop a file, or `⇧⌘I`.
- **Find anything.** `⌘P` in a note, or `⌃⌥⌘F` from any app, searches every note by what it says. Pin the ones you keep reaching for — `⌘1` to `⌘9` open them.
- **Name a note.** Double-click its title to call it something other than its first line.
- **Lock a note.** Lock note keeps one read-only, so a stray key or click cannot change it. Unlock it to edit.
- **Nothing lost.** A deleted note waits 30 days in Recently Deleted.
- **Your colours.** Yellow, blue, green, pink, purple or orange, on glass or on paper.

## Private by design

- Notes are kept on this Mac, encrypted with a key Lumi holds in your Keychain.
- Nothing you write leaves your Mac. The only thing fetched is a picture you paste from a web page — once, to keep it with the note.
- Notes are Markdown underneath: Copy as Markdown takes one anywhere.

## Getting started

1. Install, and press `⌃⌥⌘N`.
2. Type a title, then anything.
3. `⌘P` to find it again, `⌘D` to leave it on the desktop.

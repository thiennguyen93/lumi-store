Lumi Gõ tiếng Việt types Vietnamese on your Mac with Telex or VNI, the keys you already know, and no input method to install. Type `tieengs` and it is *tiếng* before you reach the space.

[Watch the demo on YouTube](https://youtu.be/qhMTPz81R3w).

## What you get

- **Telex or VNI.** `tieengs` and `tie61ng` both make *tiếng*. Pick one in the popover or in Settings.
- **A V in the menu bar.** Click it for everything: on or off, Telex or VNI, and the options below. It turns to an E while you type English.
- **fn ⇧ to switch.** Press fn and ⇧ together and let go, and you are typing English; again, and you are back to Vietnamese. A right-click on the V does the same, and Settings takes another shortcut if you prefer one.
- **Each app keeps its own.** Switch to English in Terminal once and it stays English there, while Messages stays Vietnamese.
- **Keep English.** Words like *Instagram* and *Order* come back exactly as you typed them, while the Vietnamese around them is typed as Vietnamese. One switch in the popover.
- **Escape gives a word back.** Press `esc` while typing a word to get back the keys you pressed: *tiếng* becomes `tieengs`.
- **Modern tone marks**, *hoà* and *thuý*, or the older style, *hòa* and *thúy*.
- **In Tiếng Việt or English.** Every page of it reads in the language you choose.

## Private by design

- **Works offline.** It has no network access, so nothing you type leaves your Mac.
- **No typing history.** It keeps no keys. All it remembers is whether you switched Vietnamese on or off in each app.
- **Only the word you are typing.** Lumi lets it change the word before the caret, and nothing else.
- **Open source.** Every line of it can be read.

## Getting started

1. Install, then choose your language and typing method in the welcome tour.
2. Start typing. The V in the menu bar says Vietnamese is on.
3. Press fn ⇧ for English, and again for Vietnamese.

Needs Lumi 1.62 or later. More about extensions at [lumikeys.app](https://lumikeys.app/docs/extensions).

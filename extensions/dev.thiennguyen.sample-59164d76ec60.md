The reference extension for anyone building on Lumi: one of everything an extension can add, each small enough to read in one sitting.

## What it shows

- **An action** you can put on a shortcut, *Translate selection*, with options for the language and what to translate.
- **A flow node**, *Shout*, that upper-cases each item it is given.
- **A setting**, stored through Lumi and read back by the extension.
- **Windows** of its own, and its own About and Settings pages.

## Who it is for

Install it to see how an extension looks and behaves inside Lumi, then read its source next to the docs. It asks for the clipboard, the selection, the apps you have open and the network, so the review sheet has something to show.

Build your own: [the extension guide](https://lumikeys.app/docs/extensions).

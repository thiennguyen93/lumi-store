## What you get

Select text with the mouse — in a browser, a document, a chat, anywhere — and a small bar of actions shows up right beside it. It offers what fits what you selected:

- **Copy** and **Search** for anything — Search shows the results right in the bar, or in your browser if you'd rather.
- **Open link** when it is a web address, **New email** when it is an email address.
- **The answer** when it is a sum: select `1280 * 0.15 + 42` and the bar says `= 234`. Press it to write the answer in.
- **Format JSON** when it is JSON.
- **Case**, **Lines** and **Encode**: UPPERCASE, Title Case, camelCase, Sort lines, Remove duplicate lines, Base64, URL encode, Slugify and more. The change is written straight over what you selected and stays selected, so you can make another.
- **Count** the words, characters and lines.
- **Translate** into your own language, on a card beside the text: translated on this Mac by macOS, or by the AI you set up in Lumi, which is better at tone and idioms. Change either language on the card, then Copy or Replace.
- **Define** a word or a phrase from the dictionaries you turned on in the Dictionary app: how it is said, with Speak, what it means, examples, synonyms, and translations from a bilingual dictionary such as Vietnamese – English. A tab for each dictionary, and one press to open the word in the Dictionary app.
- **With the AI you set up in Lumi**: **Fix** spelling and grammar, **Rewrite** in the style you pick, **Summarize**, **Explain**, **Ask…** a question about it, **Reply…** to a message — it offers the ways you might answer, and writes the one you pick — or add actions of your own. Each runs the instructions you see and can change in Settings, on the model you choose; a card can ask again with another of your models, or with more thinking. A fix or a rewrite is shown with what changed, for you to Replace; a reply or an explanation is shown for you to Copy — an answer's headings, lists, code and tables drawn as they are meant to read.

The bar never takes the keyboard from the app you are in, and it goes away the moment you click, type or scroll somewhere else.

## From the keyboard

Press **⌃⌥⌘Space** with text selected for the same bar, in any app — handy where you selected with the keyboard. Numbers or arrow keys choose, Return presses, Escape closes.

## Yours to arrange

Choose which actions show and in what order, icons alone or with their names, and the search engine and where its results show. Hold **⌥** for a second bar of actions you choose, so more fit without going under ⋯. Keep the bar from popping up in an app with **Don't pop up in…** under ⋯ on the bar, or have it come up only with the shortcut — or hold ⌘ as you select to keep it away that once.

## Private by design

What you select is read by Lumi on this Mac and handed to Selection Bar, and to nothing else. It is never kept, never written to a log, and never leaves your Mac unless you press Search, or an AI action on a model that runs online, or Translate when an AI online translates — and Lumi asks you before that text goes, unless you told it not to. Translate works on this Mac by default: macOS translates the text here. The translation is never kept. Define looks the word up on this Mac, in macOS's own dictionaries, and keeps nothing. Lumi never reads a password field. To see what you select with the mouse, it only asks the app; in an app that does not answer, a button or the shortcut copies the selection with ⌘C and puts your clipboard back as it was.

## Getting started

1. Install, and the Welcome tour shows the bar at work — and says whether ⌃⌥⌘Space was free to take.
2. Select text in any app: the bar shows up beside it.
3. For the AI actions, choose a model in Lumi's Settings › AI — one on this Mac, or one online.

Needs Lumi 1.55 or later. Translate on this Mac needs macOS 26; before it, the AI you set up in Lumi translates. More about extensions at [lumikeys.app](https://lumikeys.app/docs/extensions).

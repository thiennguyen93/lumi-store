Screenshot captures an area, a window or the whole screen with one key and opens it in an editor to mark it up — then it is copied, saved, or dragged straight into another app.

[Watch the demo on YouTube](https://youtu.be/3xeg8odiBO8).

## What you get

- **A key for every capture.** `⌘⇧2` an area, `⌥⌘⇧2` a window you point at, `⌘⇧1` the whole screen, `⌥⌘⇧1` the window in front. Change any of them in Settings.
- **An editor made for pointing things out.** Arrows, lines, boxes, ellipses, a pen, a marker, callouts, text and numbered steps, each on a key of its own. Crop, rotate, spotlight and pixelate, then finish on a backdrop with a shadow.
- **Several pictures at once**, as tabs, each keeping its own marks and undo.
- **Copy, save or drag it out.** PNG or JPEG, at full resolution or screen size, named the way you like, into a folder you choose.
- **Or skip the editor.** A capture can land as a card in the corner instead — copy, save, edit or flick it away — or go straight to the clipboard or a file.
- **Recent captures** with `⌘⇧0`: a card closed too soon is still there, picture and all.
- **Any picture**, not only a capture: open a file or the clipboard's picture in the editor. `⌘⇧8` opens the editor.

## The words in a picture

- **Hide what's private.** Select text (`E`) reads the picture's own words. One click pixelates every email address and phone number in it, or drag across exactly the words to hide.
- **Copy text from anything.** Press `⌘⇧9` and drag over words you can't select — a video, a picture, a locked app — and they are on the clipboard, Vietnamese included. Double-click a line on the card to copy just that one.
- **Copy from a capture, too.** Select text picks a letter at a time, a word with a double-click, everything with `⌘A`.

## Private by design

- Lumi takes the picture, and only when you press a capture key.
- The words are read by macOS, on this Mac.
- No network access: nothing you capture leaves your Mac.
- The editor and the cards step out of the way while you choose, and stay out of the picture.

## Getting started

1. Install, and allow Screen Recording for Lumi when the Welcome tour asks.
2. Press `⌘⇧2` and drag over an area.
3. Mark it up, then `⌘C` to copy it or `⌘S` to save it.

Needs Lumi 1.36 or later. More about extensions at [lumikeys.app](https://lumikeys.app/docs/extensions).

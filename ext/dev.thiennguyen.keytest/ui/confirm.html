Clean the keyboard

No key will do anything, in any app, for 1 minute. Each key you wipe still lights up here.

How to unlock

  • Hold to unlock Press and hold Hold to unlock, at the top right, for 2 seconds.
  • esc+shift Or hold esc and right shift together for 2 seconds, until the ring over the keyboard empties.
  • Or wait — it unlocks by itself when the time runs out.
  • Touch ID locks your Mac, which ends cleaning too.

How long, and whether the trackpad is locked too, are in the Settings tab.

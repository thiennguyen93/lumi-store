Lock every key on your Mac while you wipe the keyboard, right inside Lumi — then see that every key still works. Useful for a quick clean, after a spill, before buying a used Mac, or when one key seems to type twice.

## Clean your keyboard

**Clean keyboard**, in the **Keyboard** tab this adds to Lumi, locks every key on your Mac at once, so you can wipe the keyboard without typing into anything. Brightness, volume, media, Mission Control and Spotlight keys are held too, and so is the trackpad, so a wipe across it clicks nothing. In the **Settings** tab, choose how long — 30 seconds to 5 minutes — and whether the trackpad is locked as well.

- **Wipe and test at once.** Each key you wipe lights up, so when you are done you know which keys work and which type twice.
- **Ways out.** Hold **esc** and **right shift** together for 2 seconds, or let the time run out. With the trackpad left unlocked, holding the unlock button for 2 seconds works too.
- **Touch ID can't be locked.** Pressing it locks your Mac, which ends cleaning.

## Test every key

1. Open the **Keyboard** tab this adds to Lumi.
2. Press each key and watch it light up on the drawn keyboard.
3. The count shows how many keys you have covered, and which are held right now.

## What it catches

- **Double-typing.** A key that registers twice from one press is flagged. That chatter is what a worn or dusty switch produces.
- **Dead keys.** Anything you pressed that never lit up is easy to spot.
- **The awkward ones.** `fn` / Globe and `F1`–`F12` are heard too, even the ones macOS normally keeps for itself.

## Good to know

- ANSI (US) or ISO layout, to match the Mac in front of you, in Lumi's light or dark theme.
- Cleaning asks Lumi to hold your keyboard, which the install sheet tells you before you add it. Nothing leaves the page.

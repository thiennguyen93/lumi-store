A rhythm game for the keys under your fingers. Notes fall to the beat of a song you chose; press D F J K as they cross the line. It plays in a window of its own, opened from **Play Rhythm Keys** in Lumi's menu bar menu.

## Your songs, your library

Rhythm Keys comes with no music. Add the songs you want to play:

- **Free music.** Search openly licensed songs from Jamendo, ccMixter and more, through Openverse. Listen before you add one; each keeps its licence, and Rhythm Keys shows its credit.
- **From your Mac.** Choose MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF or FLAC files. The originals stay where they are.
- **From a link.** Any direct link to an audio file you are allowed to use.

Each song is read on your Mac as it is added: its tempo, its beats and where its notes fall. From that, Rhythm Keys writes its charts for you, at four difficulties. Group songs into collections, star your favourites, and choose a song to hear its liveliest fifteen seconds.

## Playing

- **Easy to Expert**, with 4, 6 or 7 keys: D F J K, S D F J K L, or Space in the middle as well.
- **Taps and holds**, judged Perfect, Great, Good or Miss, with how early or late you were.
- **Results** show your grade, accuracy, longest combo and which way your timing leans, with an offset to fix it in one click.
- **A daily song** from your library, sometimes mirrored, with a streak to keep.
- **Esc pauses.** Hide the window and the game pauses too.

## Good to know

- Change the keys, the scroll speed and the volumes in Settings, and calibrate your audio offset by tapping along.
- Songs stay on your Mac, in Rhythm Keys' own storage. The install sheet tells you it reaches the internet: that is the free music search and the downloads you ask for.

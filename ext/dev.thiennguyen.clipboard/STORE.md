Clipboard Manager remembers what you copy on this Mac and brings it back with one shortcut: search, pick, and it is pasted into the app you were typing in.

## What you get

- **One shortcut.** `⌘⇧C` opens the panel over whatever you are working in.
- **Everything you copy.** Text, rich text, links, colours, files and images, each with a preview.
- **Search as you type**, including the text inside copied screenshots.
- **Pins** for the things you keep reaching for, kept at the top.
- **Drag an item out** into any app, or save a copied image as a file.

## Private by design

- Anything a password manager marks as secret is **never recorded**. Lumi drops it before the extension hears of it.
- The history is stored encrypted, with a key Lumi keeps in your Keychain.
- No network access: nothing you copy leaves your Mac.
- Leave out the apps you never want kept, and choose how long history is kept.

## Getting started

1. Install, then press `⌘⇧C`.
2. Type to narrow the list, or use the arrow keys.
3. Press Return, and the item is pasted where you were.

Everything else is in the extension's Settings tab. More about extensions at [lumikeys.app](https://lumikeys.app/docs/extensions).

A steady beat to practise with, right inside Lumi. Pick a tempo, a time signature and a sound, accent the first beat, and keep what you set as a preset for next time.

## Set the beat

- **Tempo** from 20 to 300 beats a minute: − and + (hold **⇧** for 10), the slider, the **←** and **→** keys, a double-click on the number to type it in, or tap it in with **Tap** or **T**.
- **Time signature**: 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8, 7/8 and 12/8, or one of your own — any number of beats of half, quarter, eighth or sixteenth notes.
- **Subdivision**: two, three or four clicks inside each beat.
- **Accent** the first beat of every bar, louder and higher. With Beat Pads, accent any beat or leave it silent.
- **Sound**: Click, Woodblock, Beep, Wind-up, Cowbell, Hi-hat, Sticks or Clave, and a volume of its own.

## Come back to it

Press **＋** at the foot of the tab to keep what is set now as a preset, and click it next time to have it all back. A preset lights up while what you have set is exactly it. Double-click one to name it; right-click one to rename it, replace it with what is set now, or delete it — a delete can be undone.

Whatever you set last is there the next time you open the tab, presets or not.

## Four ways to see it

Choose in the **Settings** tab, or at the foot of the Metronome tab while it plays:

- **Classic** — a wind-up pendulum that swings to each beat. Drag its weight to change the tempo.
- **Orbit** — the bar as a ring, a hand sweeping round it. Turn the ring to change the tempo.
- **Beat Pads** — one pad per beat, lit as it plays. Click a pad to accent it or leave it silent.
- **Simple** — plain settings rows, with a card that flashes on the beat.

## Good to know

- The beat is kept on the Mac's audio clock, so it stays steady however busy the Mac is.
- **Space** starts and stops. With Reduce Motion on, nothing swings or sweeps — the beat is shown by light alone.
- It asks for nothing, and nothing leaves this Mac.
- The beat plays while the Metronome tab is open, and stops when you leave it or close Settings.

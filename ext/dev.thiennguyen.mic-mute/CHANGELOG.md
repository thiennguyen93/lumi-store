# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-09

### Added

- **⌥⌘M mutes every microphone on your Mac** — built-in, USB and Bluetooth — and pressing it again puts each one back as it was. A right-click on the menu bar icon, or **Mute Every Microphone** in its menu, does the same.
- **A microphone you plug in while muted is muted too**, and so is one unmuted somewhere else, until you unmute from Mic Mute.
- **The menu bar icon says which it is**: a microphone, or a microphone crossed out.
- **A microphone Lumi cannot mute is named**, such as an iPhone used as a microphone, so it is never taken for muted.
- **Mute virtual microphones too**, in Settings, off by default: meeting apps' own virtual microphones are left alone unless you turn it on.
- Needs Lumi 1.77.0.

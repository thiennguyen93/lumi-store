## The Vietnamese word list

The engine carries a list of Vietnamese syllables (`core/src/data/dictionaries/vi.dic`
in Gõ Nhanh), which comes from the hunspell-vi dictionaries
(https://github.com/1ec5/hunspell-vi). Those were converted by László Németh
from the GNU Aspell Vietnamese dictionary
(https://ftp.gnu.org/gnu/aspell/dict/vi/), whose word list is by Hồ Ngọc Đức
and is distributed under the GNU General Public License. That is why this
extension is distributed under the GPL too.

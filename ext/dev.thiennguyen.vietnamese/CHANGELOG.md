# Changelog

## [0.1.1] - 2026-10-22

### Added

- The Settings tab reads in Vietnamese or English: choose with **Display language** at its top. Vietnamese is the default.
- The row in Lumi's menu bar menu follows the display language: **Gõ tiếng Việt**, or **Vietnamese typing** in English.
- An About tab of its own, in the display language: what the extension can reach, who made it and how to get in touch, its page in the store, its source, and its licence and third-party notices.
- The Settings and About tab names follow the display language: **Cài đặt** and **Giới thiệu** in Vietnamese.

### Changed

- The Settings tab is laid out as rows, each with its control on the right: the typing method is a Telex/VNI switch, and placing tone marks the modern way (hoà, thuý) is a switch of its own.
- A new icon: a V over a keyboard.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-21

### Added

- Type Vietnamese in every app with Telex or VNI, switched on and off from Lumi's menu bar or a shortcut you choose for **Switch Vietnamese typing on or off**.
- The switch is remembered for each app, unless you turn that off in Settings.
- Escape gives back the keys as you typed them, and English words can be kept as typed — both in Settings.
